Data & privacy

Meetings, transcripts and audio live in IndexedDB on this device. Deleting a meeting removes its audio too. No remote code runs in this extension. Nothing is uploaded except the model requests you configure above.

What this can and cannot capture
  • Supported: any Chrome tab that plays audio — Google Meet, Zoom web, Teams web, a recorded call, a video.
  • Supported: your microphone, on its own or mixed with tab audio.
  • Not supported: native desktop apps (Zoom desktop, Teams desktop). Chrome's capture API only sees tabs.
  • Not supported: speaker diarization. Labels are never guessed — you can correct speakers in the transcript.
  • Local servers: an Ollama or LM Studio endpoint needs that origin allowed once; Chrome prompts on save.
