Get to your first answer

Three steps. Nothing leaves this device except the model request you configure.

  1. 1

    Connect an AI provider

    Bring your own key — OpenAI, Anthropic, Gemini, or a local model. Summaries, decisions and answers all run on it.

    Add key
  2. 2

    Record a meeting

    Open a meeting tab (Meet, Zoom web, Teams web), then click the MeetDolff icon and choose Tab + mic so you capture both sides of the call.

  3. 3

    Turn audio into a transcript

    Pick Cloud Whisper in Settings for recorded audio, or keep Web Speech for live captions. Then Summarize in the side panel.
